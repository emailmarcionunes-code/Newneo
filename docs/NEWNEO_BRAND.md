# NEWNEO visual direction

Approved September 16, 2026.

- White NEWNEO wordmark on dark backgrounds; no decorative green dot.
- Solid petroleum blue #245563 for primary actions, links and selected states; hover #193F4B.
- Marketing site: neutral graphite #101114 / #17181C, white #F5F5F7 and neutral gray supporting text. Generous spacing, restrained borders, no metallic backgrounds, glows or decorative card movement.
- Platform: retain light work surfaces and dense, readable operational tables. Platform action and selection styles also use petroleum blue #245563, with pale petroleum backgrounds. Semantic status colors and official artwork are retained. Green, amber and red communicate success, warning and error states.
- Keyboard focus remains visible; reduced-motion preferences are respected.
