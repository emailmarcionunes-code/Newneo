# Newneo IP and Contract Principles

## Global contract structure
Newneo should operate from a global contractual framework with jurisdiction-specific schedules where local law requires them.

Target structure:
- Global Master Services Agreement
- Order Form
- Platform Terms
- Data Processing Addendum
- Security Schedule
- Service Level terms
- jurisdiction-specific schedules as needed
- project Statement of Work when applicable

## Ownership principle
Customer-specific assets and Newneo reusable assets must remain clearly separated.

Customer-owned or customer-controlled categories include:
- customer data
- customer confidential information
- customer documents and knowledge
- customer business rules and policies
- customer credentials and system access

Newneo-owned categories include:
- Newneo platform
- reusable skills
- agent templates
- generic connectors and patterns
- evaluation methods
- governance frameworks
- orchestration patterns
- deployment automation
- AgentOps methods
- generalized improvements and know-how

## Reusable IP
Reusable IP means intellectual property that can be used across customers without exposing customer-specific confidential information.

Newneo will continuously improve its platform and reusable intelligence from generalized operational experience.

The source of an improvement may be cumulative across many deployments and may not be attributable to one customer. Generalized improvements remain Newneo assets provided they do not disclose or transfer customer confidential information.

Principle:
**Customer-specific knowledge stays customer-specific. Generalizable intelligence becomes Newneo reusable IP.**

## Skills and accumulated intelligence
Skills may originate from open standards, public patterns, internal development or customer projects.

The competitive asset is not merely the existence of a skill. Newneo value comes from hardening, validation, evaluations, governance, enterprise patterns, production evidence and operating experience.

## Exit and termination principle
Leaving Newneo should be possible without transferring ownership of the Newneo platform.

The customer should be able to receive its applicable data and agreed customer-specific artifacts. Newneo platform software, reusable IP and generalized intelligence remain Newneo property.

For private deployments, the product should support controlled license expiration and a defined transition/offboarding process rather than destructive shutdown behavior.

## Contract drafting note
These principles are product and commercial requirements, not final legal language. Final contract language must be reviewed for the relevant jurisdiction by qualified counsel.
