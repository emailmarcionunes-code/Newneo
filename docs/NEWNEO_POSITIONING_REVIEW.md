## NEWNEO positioning

NEWNEO helps enterprise teams turn a business workflow into an agent with reusable, versioned skills. Teams define knowledge, tools, controls and acceptance criteria in one workspace, then expand capability through reviewed versions. Scoped engineering supports integration and production readiness.

Portuguese: A NEWNEO ajuda empresas a transformar processos em agentes com skills reutilizáveis e versionadas. A equipe define conhecimento, ferramentas, controles e critérios de sucesso em um ambiente comum, e amplia as capacidades por versões revisadas. A engenharia apoia a integração e a preparação para produção.

Message order: business task → agent and skills → validation and control → scoped deployment → measured expansion.

The homepage and product presentation now include an original Define / Configure / Validate / Expand section. Success metrics are evaluation criteria, not invented customer results. The current guided-pilot availability statement remains visible.
