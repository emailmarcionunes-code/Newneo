# Newneo Public Presentations & Proposal-Last Workflow

## Core decision
The formal customer proposal is the **last pre-sales artifact**. It should be practical, concise and generated only after the required commercial and technical pre-sales work is complete.

The objective is to reduce friction while still covering the information required to make a responsible commitment.

## Pre-sales sequence
`Lead → Qualification → Discovery → Paid Assessment(s) → Solution Review → Scope & Commercial Review → Proposal Ready → Proposal`

Customer-facing engagement language remains:
`Discover → Assess → Prove → Deploy → Operate`

The proposal is not used to discover the opportunity. It packages decisions that have already been made and validated.

## Public HTML presentations
Newneo maintains standard public HTML presentations on the website.

These presentations are:
- public information
- indexable and accessible from the public website
- a customer education resource
- the standard reference used by sellers, SREs, architects and other Newneo employees
- designed for screen sharing during customer conversations
- designed so a customer can review them independently before or after a meeting
- version-controlled rather than manually rebuilt as PowerPoint decks

Initial public presentation:
- `/presentations/engagement`

Future public presentation library may include:
- Newneo Overview
- How We Engage
- AI Infrastructure Readiness
- AI Compute Strategy
- Agent Factory
- Enterprise RAG
- MCP & Enterprise Actions
- AI Security & Governance
- AgentOps
- Industry-specific overviews
- Assessment-specific overviews

## Public presentation principle
**One standard story for everyone.**

The public presentation is the canonical explanation of Newneo's methodology. Sales and technical teams should not recreate the same narrative in separate decks.

The standard public presentation should be usable in three ways:
1. A website visitor reads it independently.
2. A seller or architect uses it live with a customer.
3. A customer-specific opportunity presentation reuses its standard sections and adds approved opportunity data.

## Customer-specific opportunity presentation
A specific opportunity may have a dynamic HTML presentation generated by Newneo Platform.

It inherits the standard public presentation structure and adds only customer-safe opportunity information such as:
- customer challenge
- what Newneo understood
- current engagement stage
- completed steps
- assessment findings approved for customer visibility
- recommended architecture
- recommended next step
- POC scope/results when applicable
- open customer decisions
- proposal status

It must never expose internal-only fields such as margin, competitive notes, internal probability, internal risk notes or approval comments.

## Engagement progress
All engagement presentations use the same process bar:
`Discover → Assess → Prove → Deploy → Operate`

Visual state:
- completed: completed/check state
- current: Newneo green `#60d394`
- future: neutral

This allows the customer and Newneo team to immediately understand where the engagement is.

## Proposal-last rule
`Generate Proposal` remains unavailable until every required pre-sales gate is complete.

Required information should include, according to engagement type:
- customer and sponsor
- business problem
- desired outcome
- current state
- relevant assessment(s)
- findings
- recommended approach
- architecture where required
- scope
- deliverables
- responsibilities
- assumptions
- exclusions
- dependencies
- risks
- success / acceptance criteria
- delivery effort
- schedule
- pricing
- commercial approvals
- applicable legal/commercial terms
- optional training/enablement package

The system should request only information relevant to the specific engagement. Avoid unnecessary mandatory fields that create administrative friction.

## Practical proposal design
The final proposal should be concise by default.

Recommended structure:
1. Executive Summary
2. What We Understood
3. Recommended Engagement / Solution
4. Scope & Deliverables
5. Customer / Newneo Responsibilities
6. Assumptions & Key Dependencies
7. Success / Acceptance Criteria
8. Timeline
9. Commercials
10. Optional Training / Enablement
11. Next Steps

Detailed technical findings, architecture documents, assessment reports or test evidence should be linked or attached rather than repeated in full inside the commercial proposal.

## Friction reduction principles
- Ask once; reuse everywhere.
- Use conditional fields and checklists based on opportunity type.
- Do not require a meeting when information can be consumed asynchronously.
- Use meetings for decisions, clarification, design, approval and negotiation.
- Allow customer review through HTML presentation links.
- Generate documents from structured data.
- Do not require sellers to rebuild standard presentations.
- Do not ask sellers to re-enter information already captured by CRM or assessments.
